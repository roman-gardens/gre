## Garden Description
Situated in the suburban quarter on the right bank of the Rhone, 160 meters west of the river, the house occupied an area estimated at more than 1175 square meters.  It was the subject of a salvage excavation in 1967. To judge by the pavements, it was occupied at the beginning of the third century, though previous phases are probable. The incomplete plan of the house is linear. Probably, one entered at the southwest corner, followed a narrow corridor for about ten meters and then turned left (north) into a porch [1] followed by a larger, square room [2] that may have been a vestibule. Farther north, on the axis of this room, was a peristyle with 2.5 meter-wide galleries around four sides of a garden [9] (about 17 x 10.5 m). The garden was surrounded on three sides (west, south, and east) by a U shaped pool with arms 1.3 meters wide and with a little apse in the middle of the south side. There was also a doorway into the northwest corner of the peristyle, directly from the street.  It was perhaps a service entrance. A second peristyle and garden farther north are possible, but our knowledge of the area is very scanty.
