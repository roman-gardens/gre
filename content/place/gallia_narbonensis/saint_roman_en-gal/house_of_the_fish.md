## Garden Description
<!-- This is the main text describing the garden -->

The little street of the Gardens (la ruelle des Jardins) runs along the north side of the House of Sucellus. On its north side, opposite the eastern end of the House of Sucellus, was the House of the Fish, about 90 meters back from the river. It was uncovered during programmed excavations in 1989 and occupies more than 2000 square meters. It was constructed around A.D. 60 over previous building. It underwent several remodelings up to the middle of the third century. Only the south wing is known. This residential part was arranged along a court, connected to the street on the south by long corridor. The peristyle [1] was partly a garden [a], along the west portico. A large pool [b], which has been partially excavated, ran along the south portico opposite a big reception room [3] (7 x 11 m). In a polychrome mosaic lining the bottom of the pool, fish, crustaceans, and mollusks stand out on aquatic waves.
