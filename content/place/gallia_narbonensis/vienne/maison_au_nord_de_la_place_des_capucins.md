## Garden Description

In 1748, north of Place des Capucins, inside the ancient roman walls, ruins of a building were found that L. Charvet describes as ancient baths, which, from his description and without a known or surviving plan, appears to be the remains of a peristyle domus. A rectangular basin measuring 11 by 3 meters featured exedrae extending from three of its sides: it adorned a peristyle with 4-meter-wide porticoes, lined with columns of violet marble. Access to the garden is through two-stepped stairs situated in front of the porticoe. In 1778, multiple mosaics were found in the surrounding environment that confirmed the precense of one or more dwellings featuring high-quality decor.

During the excavation, traces of a violent fire on the walls of the basin and among the unearthed remains (mosaic fragments, charcoal, scorched stones, molten lead) were found. However, no dating of the site is given.
