## Garden Description
<!-- Situated in the Place Camille Jouffray just south of the railway station, this group of residential structures was dug out between 1974 and 1981. Where artisans’ shops had been in Augustan and pre-Augustan times, a house was built around A.D. 10-20. It was subsequently replaced by a building with a courtyard. In the time of Hadrian, two neighboring houses were built which lasted to the middle of the third century. The one on the east had a peristyle court [12] surrounded by a channel pool with an apse in the center of the south side. The one on the west had a courtyard with a rectangular pool and an apse facing a reception room [7]. Gardens were presumably planted in both courtyards. In the second half of the second century, the two houses were reunited and the peristyle [12] disappeared. -->
