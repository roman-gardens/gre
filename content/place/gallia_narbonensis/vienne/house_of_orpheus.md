## Garden Description
<!-- This is the main text describing the garden -->
This house in the Champ de Mars was partially excavated between 1859 and 1880 and is known only from reports of that time. It is organized in two blocks of building on either side of a peristyle [1] with a garden presumably in the center. A channel pool bordered the three known sides of the peristyle and  may have run along the last side as well. The west side of this pool was ornamented with an apse off center towards the north. It was perhaps lined up with the opening of the reception area [6]. To the east, the large room [10] opened through a bay 3.2 meters wide. This room, decorated with the mosaic of Orpheus, has been interpreted by A. Pelletier as a *frigidarium* with a bath but by A. Bouet as a *triclinium*. For André Pelletier, this house was only part of a larger house extending to the south.
