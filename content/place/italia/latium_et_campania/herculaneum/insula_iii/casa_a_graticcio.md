## Excavation Date

1927-1929

## Garden Description

This multi-family dwelling was excavated in 1927-1929. There was a small paved courtyard (a) 1.97 x 2.97 m located to provide light and air to the surrounding apartments which was enclosed on the east, north and west by a low wall 0.76 m. high which formed a catch basin for rainwater. Herbs or flowers could be grown in the planting space (14 cm wide x 25 cm deep) on the top of the wall. The middle of the north side of the wall contained the mouth of a well that was 0.90 m dia. and in the floor of the passageway to the west of the courtyard there was the mouth of a cistern covered by a circular piece of marble. There was a windlass with a rope found in a nearby room undoubtedly used to draw water from either of these fixtures. According to Maiuri, there could be a large cistern underground connecting the mouth of cistern in the passageway and the mouth of the well.
