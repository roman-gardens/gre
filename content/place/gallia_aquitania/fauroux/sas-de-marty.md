## Garden Description
Fauroux is about 35 kilometers east of Agen.  The site was at the place called Sas-de-Marty, near the church in Saint-Romain, 2 kilometers east of Farroux, on the right bank of the Séoune, on the hillside. (There is no plan and no map in Demeures.) It has been known since 1852. Mosaics were found in 1950 and removed to museums in 1977. The villa had a vast gallery ( 50 or perhaps 80 x 3.5 m) covered with mosaics; in view of the topography, it was probably a façade.  The decoration is very rich; there are stuccoes with vine motifs, and one room with three rectangular arms has preserved a decorative painting of a garden fence on the wall of the central arm (Demeures, fig. 80, p.205)  All these elements suggest that one is here in the presence of a rich residence of late antiquity.
