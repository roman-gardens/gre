## Garden Description
<!-- This is the main text describing the garden -->
The garden was exposed only for 6.1 by 9 meters, but its length is thought to have been more than 20 meters.  A low wall, 60 centimeters high, with a rounded top bore the 4.45 meter high colonnade. The walls of the gallery were painted; rectangular red panels alternated with narrower, vertical black panels enhanced by wreaths and chains of foliage and fruits, cupids, masques, and birds.  The lower register was a sequence of wild animals.
