## Garden Description
<!-- This is the main text describing the garden -->
Discovered during excavation of Boulevard Gambetta, this house, in its original phase, had a central room with a painted décor of dolphins and aquatic motifs.  In a second phase, towards the middle of the second century, this room [1] was transformed into a light well.  It was a court open to the sky, though somewhat protected by the projection of a roof.  One band of the former floor was kept; all the rest was covered by hauled-in earth, perhaps for planting.  In the northwest corner of the court were found two vats for crushing lime.
