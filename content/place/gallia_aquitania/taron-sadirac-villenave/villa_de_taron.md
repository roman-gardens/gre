## Garden Description
<!-- This is the main text describing the garden -->
Vestiges of this villa in the valley of the Petit Lées are found in an area of some 2.5 hectares around the Romanesque church built over the southern part of the villa. It sat upon  a terrace with a gentle slope towards the east. The church kept the same orientation as the villa under it, which is almost exactly true north-south and east-west.  The remains of the villa were noted in the eighteenth century.  Excavations conducted in 1995-96 by Ph. Vergain were able to ascertain an original construction in the first century.  According to Vergain, a remodeling in the third century created the peristyle which may have been planted as a garden.  Four phases have been detected for late antiquity; the mosaic decorations come from the last.
